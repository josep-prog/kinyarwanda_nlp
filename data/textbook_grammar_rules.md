## PART 1: UTUREMAJAMBO TW'IZINA (Noun Morphemes)

### 1.1 Intego y'izina mbonera gakondo (Basic Simple Noun Structure)

**Formula: D + RT + C**

| Component | Kinyarwanda term | Description |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------|
| D | Indomo | The initial vowel (prefix) of the noun. Only three vowels can be indomo: i, u, a |
| RT | Indanganteko (indangasano) | The class marker; determines agreement with other words in the sentence |
| C | Igicumbi | The stem; does not change; carries the core meaning |

**Ingero (Examples):**
- `i-ki-vuguto` → D=i, RT=ki (nt.7), C=vuguto
- `a-ma-saka` → D=a, RT=ma (nt.6), C=saka
- `u-mu-vure` → D=u, RT=mu (nt.3), C=vure
- `a-ba-na` → D=a, RT=ba (nt.2), C=na (but: u→w/-J rule applies: umwana)
- `i-bi-hugu` → D=i, RT=bi (nt.8), C=hugu

**Notes:**
- Izina mbonera gakondo rishobora kutagira indomo
- Rishobora kutagira indanganteko (then gahagararirwa n'iki kimenyetso: ø)
- NTIRISHOBORA kubura igicumbi

### 1.2 Indomo (Initial Vowel)
Inyajwi zishobora kuba indomo: **i, u, a**

Indomo ya "a" → appears in classes 2 (aba-), 4 (imi-), 6 (ama-), 12 (aka-), 14 (ubu-)
Indomo ya "u" → appears in classes 1 (umu-), 3 (umu-), 11 (uru-), 14 (ubu-), 15 (uku-)
Indomo ya "i" → appears in classes 5 (i-), 7 (iki-), 8 (ibi-), 9 (in-), 10 (in-)

### 1.3 Amategeko y'igenamajwi akoreshwa mu izina (Phonological rules for nouns)

Amategeko y'igenamajwi yandikwa mu mpine (written using notation in the analyses).

Key rules (from S3 and S4 analyses):
- `u → w / _J` (u becomes w before another vowel): umwana = u-mu-ana (u→w/_J)
- `u → ø / _J` (u drops before vowel in some contexts): abana = a-ba-ana (u→ø/_J)
- `i → y / _J` (i becomes y before vowel): umwana w'inka
- `i → e / _Co-` (i becomes e after nasal+consonant cluster): i-n-bon-ik-a → i→e/-Co-
- `n → m / _b` (n assimilates before b): impuza = i-n-huza → n→m/-b (via mp)
- `n → m / _h` (n → m before h: then mh → mp in orthography): impuzamiryango

Example morphological parse (from S3):
```
Umucasuka:   u – mu – ci – a – ø – ø – suka     (i→ø/-J)
Nyiricyubahiro:  nyiri – ø – ki – ubah – ir – o   (i→y/-J, ky→cy mu nyandiko, i→ø/-J)
Nyiramahirwe:  nyira – ø – ma – hir – w – e       (i→ø/-J)
Ubwirakabiri:  u – bu – ir – a – ka – biri        (u→w/-J)
Imbonekarimwe: i – n – bon – ik – a – ri – mwe    (n→m/-b, i→e/-Co-)
Impuzamiryango: i – n – hur – y – a – ø – mi – ryango  (n→m/-h, mh→mp, r+y→z)
Insina z'amatwi: i – n – tsina – zi – ø – a – ma – twi  (t→ø/n-s, i→ø/-J, a→ø/-J)
```

## PART 2: AMAZINA Y'URUSOBE (Compound/Derived Nouns)

### 2.1 Inshoza (Definition)
Izina ry'urusobe ni izina rimwe ariko rigizwe n'amagambo arenze rimwe CYANGWA agizwe n'uturemajambo turenze dutatu tw'ibanze. Iyo risesenguwe rigira:
- Indomo ebyiri
- Indangazina ebyiri
- Ibicumbi bibiri
- Cyangwa: izina ryoroheje + utubimbura/imisuma/ingereka

### 2.2 Amoko y'amazina y'urusobe

| Type | Kinyarwanda | Description | Examples |
|------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | Amazina y'inyunge | In meaning only; in writing the 2nd explains the 1st (becomes adjective-like) | (inyunge compounds) |
| 2 | Amazina y'urujyanonshinga | Verb + its complement fused into one noun | Iterambere (gutera+imbere), Ingaramakirambi |
| 3 | Amazina y'akabimbura | Noun prefixed with a small affix (akabimbura) | Nyakubahwa, Nyiricyubahiro, Umunyarwanda, Semasaka |
| 4 | Amazina y'imisuma | Noun with suffix added at end | Umwamikazi, Umugabekazi, Igitakazi, Umugabazi |
| 5 | Amazina ahujwe n'ikinyazina ngenera | Two nouns joined by associative (ngenera) forming single meaning | Insina z'amatwi, Amaso y'ikirayi, Inkondo y'umura |
| 6 | Amazina y'impindura rwego | Words from other classes that shift into noun class | Kwibuka (verb→noun), Irambona (verb→noun), Sibomana |

### 2.3 Utubimbura (Noun Prefixes)
Common utubimbura in Kinyarwanda: **a, ka, nya, nyiri, nyira, se, sa, -ene**

| Prefix | Usage | Example |
|--------|-------|---------|
| nya- | associative/ownership | Nyagahene, Nyamvura |
| nyiri- | owner/possessor | Nyiri ubwenge, Nyiricyubahiro |
| nyira- | mother-of / female assoc. | Nyirabukwe, Nyiramahirwe |
| se- / sa- | father-of / male assoc. | Semasaka, Samvura |
| ka- | diminutive/familiar | Kanyana, Kamana, Kagabo |
| a- (mu) | person from | Umunyarwanda = u-mu-nya-rwanda |
| -ene | reflexive/own | Mwenese |

### 2.4 Imisuma (Noun Suffixes)

| Suffix | Meaning | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| -kazi | female | umwamikazi, umugabekazi, umugabazi (→gabo+kazi phonologically), igitakazi |
| -azi | female (variant) | umugabazi |
| -rume | male relative/aspect | (marume, nyirarume) |
| -kuru | elder/senior | (sokuru, nyokuru, sekuru) |
| -kuruza | eldest | |
| -senge | paternal aunt | (masenge, nyirasenge, nyogosenge) |
| -buja | servant/subordinate | (databuja, sobuja, nyirabuja) |
| -bukwe | in-law | (databukwe, sobukwe, nyirabukwe) |

### 2.5 Inzira z'ikomorazina mvazina (Noun derivation from nouns)

**a) Ku nteko (Noun class shift — gupfobya/gutubya/gukuza):**
```
Umugabo (nt.1) → Akagabo (nt.12, diminutive)
                → Utugabo (nt.13)
                → Ubugabo (nt.14, abstract)
                → Ikigabo (nt.7, augmentative)
                → Ibigabo (nt.8, plural augmentative)
Inyana (nt.9)  → Akanyana (nt.12)
                → Urunyana (nt.11)
```

**b) Ku ndomo (dropping the class prefix → proper name):**
```
Umugabo → Mugabo
Umugisha → Mugisha
Amahirwe → Mahirwe
Akagabo → Kagabo
```

**c) Ku kwisubiramo kw'igicumbi (stem reduplication):**
```
Ikigori → Ikigorigori
Igisembe → Igisembesembe
Ikizuba → Ikizubazuba
Amatama → Amatamatama
```

**d) Ku kongera umusuma (adding suffix):**
```
Umwami + kazi → Umwamikazi
Umugabe + kazi → Umugabekazi
Umugabo + azi → Umugabazi
Igitaka + kazi → Igitakazi
```

**e) Ku kongera akabimbura (adding prefix):**
```
nya + impinga → Nyampinga
umunyа + Rwanda → Umunyarwanda
nyiri + icyubahiro → Nyiricyubahiro
se + amasaka → Semasaka
ka + inyana → Kanyana
```

## PART 3: IKOMORAZINA MVANSHINGA (Verb-Derived Nouns)

### 3.1 Inshoza
Amazina akomoka ku nshinga (verb stems). Intego y'ayo mazina: **D + RT + C + Sz** (the suffix -Sz distinguishes them).

### 3.2 Imisozo y'ikomorazina mvanshinga (derivational suffixes from verbs)

| Suffix | Examples |
|--------|---------|
| -e | impano (gift ← guha), ingemu, imboni, umutoni |
| -o | ubuhemu, ingemo, umukoro, ubucuruzi |
| -a | amazina, ibihingwa, imihigo |
| -u | ubukire, ubushobozi |
| -i | abakozi, ababaji, ikibariro |
| -yi | (used only with stems of 2+ syllables, causes phonological changes) |

**Ikitonderwa (Note on -yi vs -i):**
- Umusozo **-yi** ntukorana n'inshinga zifite igicumbi kigizwe n'umugemo umwe
- Ukorana gusa n'inshinga zifite igicumbi kigizwe n'imigemo ibiri+
- Ugatera amategeko y'igenamajwi iyo ukurikiye ingombajwi zisoza igicumbi
- Keretse iyo izo ngombajwi ari **m, b, na p** (exceptions: no phonological change)

### 3.3 Ingero z'amazina akomoka ku nshinga (S4 examples):
```
Impano, imihigo, amarushanwa, ibikorwa, ubucuruzi, ubukire, itaha, ihinga,
abakozi, umukoro, ubushobozi, akamaro, ikibariro, ababaji, abagenzi,
ubuhemu, ingemu, umutoni, imboni
```

## PART 4: UTUREMAJAMBO TW'INSHINGA (Verb Morphemes)

### 4.1 Imbundo (Infinitive/Citation Form)

**Formula: Ku – C – Soz**  (i.e., Indanganshinga + Igicumbi + Umusozo)

| Component | Term | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| Ku- (or Gu-) | Indanganshinga | Infinitive marker; marks the verb class (nteko ya 15) |
| C | Igicumbi/Umuzi | Stem; does not change; carries meaning |
| -a | Umusozo | Final vowel of infinitive (always -a for regular verbs) |

**Ingero:**
```
Gukina:    ku – kin – a
Gutsinda:  ku – tsind – a
Kuririmba: ku – ririmb – a
Gupfuka:   ku – pfuk – a
Kwiga:     ku – ig – a      (u→w/_J)
Gukorа:    ku – kor – a
```

**With tense marker (indangagihe) in infinitive:**
```
Kuzakina:  ku – za – kin – a  (za = future marker)
Kuzarya:   ku – za – ri – a
Kuzumva:   ku – za – umv – a
```

**With extensions (ingereka) between stem and ending:**
```
Guhingira: ku – hing – ir – a    (-ir = applicative/benefactive)
Guhingana: ku – hing – an – a    (-an = reciprocal)
Kuhingika:  ku – hing – ik – a   (-ik = passive/stative)
Kwigisha:  ku – ig – ish – a     (-ish/-sh = causative)
Kwigana:   ku – ig – an – a      (-an = reciprocal)
Kwigira:   ku – ig – ir – a      (-ir = applicative)
```

### 4.2 Inshoza z'ingereka z'ibanze (Basic extensions)

| Extension | Name | Meaning | Example |
|-----------|------|---------|---------|
| -an- | Ngirana | Reciprocal ("each other") | guhingana, kwigana |
| -ish-/-sh- | Ngirisha | Causative | kwigisha |
| -ir- | Ngirira | Applicative/Benefactive | guhingira |
| -w- | Ngirwa | Passive | gukorwa |
| -y- | Ngiza | Causative (alternate form) | gusetsa = gu-sek-y-a |
| -ik- | Ngirika | Stative/Potential | guhingika |
| -uk- | Ngiruka | Reversive | |
| -ur- | Ngirura | Reversive | |

### 4.3 Uko bashaka igicumbi k'inshinga (Finding the verb stem)

**Rule 1 — Verb with 2+ syllables (not all short):**
Put in ngenga ya kabiri y'ubumwe (2nd person singular imperative), remove final -a:
```
Koga → oga → igicumbi: og-
Gushaka → shaka → igicumbi: shak-
Kwiga → iga → igicumbi: ig-
Gukora → kora → igicumbi: kor-
Gushaka → shaka → igicumbi: shak-
```

**Rule 2 — Very short verb (2 open syllables, no long vowel):**
Put in mpitakare (perfective, 3sg), remove ya...ye wrapper:
```
Kujya → yagiye → igicumbi: -gi-
Kurya → yariye → igicumbi: -ri-
Gupfa → yapfuye → igicumbi: -pfu-
Gusya → yaseye → igicumbi: -se-
Kugwa → yaguye → igicumbi: -gu-
Guca → yaciye → igicumbi: -ci-
```

### 4.4 Intego mbonera y'inshinga itondaguye (Conjugated verb structure)

**Formula: Rsh – Rgh – C – Sz**

| Component | Term | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| Rsh | Indanganshinga / Indangasano ya ruhamwa | Subject agreement prefix; changes by person/class |
| Rgh | Indangagihe | Tense/aspect marker |
| C | Igicumbi | Stem |
| Sz | Umusozo | Final suffix (-a, -e, -aga, -ye) |

**Indanganshinga by person/class (from S3, table):**

Ngenga ya mbere (1st person):
- Ubumwe: n- (I)
- Ubwinshi: tu- (We)

Ngenga ya kabiri (2nd person):
- Ubumwe: u- (You sg)
- Ubwinshi: mu- (You pl)

Ngenga ya gatatu (3rd person) — varies by noun class:
- nt.1 (umuntu): a-
- nt.2 (abantu): ba-
- nt.3 (umuryango): u-
- nt.4 (imiryango): i-
- nt.5 (irari): ri-
- nt.6 (amarabi): a-
- nt.7 (ikintu): ki-
- nt.8 (ibintu): bi-
- nt.9 (inka): i-
- nt.10 (inka pl): zi-
- nt.11 (uruzi): ru-
- nt.12 (akantu): ka-
- nt.13 (utuzi): tu-
- nt.14 (ubuzi): bu-
- nt.15 (ukuzi): ku-
- nt.16 (ahantu): ha-

### 4.5 Imisozo y'inshinga (Verb endings)

| Suffix | Context |
|--------|---------|
| -a | Present/default, imperative |
| -e | Subjunctive/optative |
| -aga | Past imperfective (habitual/continuous past) |
| -ye | Perfect (past completed) |

### 4.6 Indangagihe (Tense markers in the verb)

Key tense markers (from S3 textbook table):
- **-za-** : future (inzagihe): tuzakina
- **-ra-** : present continuous/narrative: arakoze, arasome
- **-ri-** : past (used in certain past tenses)
- **-ka-** : sequential/consequential (inkurikizo): bagashaka
- **-a-** (in Rgh position) : conditional/subjunctive (inziganyo)

## PART 5: AMOKO Y'INSHINGA (Verb Classes/Types)

### 5.1 Inshinga Zisanzwe vs Inshinga Nkene/Mburabuzi

**Inshinga Zisanzwe (Regular verbs):**
- Zihinduranya mu bihe by'inshinga (conjugate through all tenses)
- Zigira imisozo mu bihe bitandukanye
- Imbundo zazo zirazwi
- Nta rujijo ziteraho
- Zigira uturemajambo tw'ibanze: indanganshinga, indangagihe, igicumbi, umusozo

**Inshinga Nkene / Mburabuzi (Defective/Irregular verbs):**
- Ntizikora nk'izisanzwe
- Ntizijya mu buryo bw'imbundo
- Ntizitondagurwa mu bihe byose
- Zubakiye ku bicumbi bidakorana n'imisozo y'inshinga
- Ibicumbi by'inshinga nkene: **-ri, -zi, -fite, -ri, -rayi...**
- Iyo zihinduriwe ibihe bikuru, ishusho yazo irahinduka; zirasimburanira n'izindi bihuje, cyangwa zikifashisha izindi nshinga nk'inyunganizi

### 5.2 Inshinga Iri mu Mbundo (Infinitive/Non-finite)
- Igaragaza igikorwa, imimerere, imiterere bitagira uwo byitirirwa
- Iteka itangizwa na "ku" y'indanganshinga (cyangwa "gu-")
- "ku" iranga inteko ya 15 (infinitive class)
- Ugira uturemajambo tw'ibanze dutatu: indanganshinga + igicumbi + umusozo (-a)

### 5.3 Inshinga Itondaguye (Finite/Conjugated Verb)
- Ihinduranya ngenga, ikaba yemeza cyangwa ihakana
- Ikumvikanisha niba igikorwa cyararangiye cyangwa gikomeza
- Ikajya mu bihe by'inshinga bitandukanye
- Igahinduranya uburyo n'ibindi

## PART 6: UBURYO BW'INSHINGA (Verb Modes)

### 6.1 Ikirango (Indicative)
Ivuga igikorwa/imimerere by'ihame. Gitondagurwa mu ndagihe, impitagihe, inzagihe.

### 6.2 Inyifurizo (Optative/Blessing)
Ivuga icyo umuntu yiyifuriza cyangwa yifuriza nyakubwirwa/nyakuvuga.
- Examples: "Urakire ukize n'abandi!" "Urakabyara hungu na kobwa."

### 6.3 Integeko (Imperative)
Ivuga itegeko rivuye kuri nyakuvuga/nyakubwirwa. Igira umusozo **-a**.
- Examples: "Jya mu ishuri isaha yageze." "Andika vuba."

### 6.4 Inkurikizo (Sequential/Consecutive)
Irangwa n'umusozo "-a-" ujyana n'indangagihe **-ka-**.
- Example: "Abana baravuka, bagakambakamba, bagakura, bakororoka, bagakura, bagasaza."

### 6.5 Ikigombero (Subjunctive/Hortative)
Bufite inyito rusange yo kuvuga ibigomba kuba/gukorwa. Umusozo **-e**.
- Examples: "Nimugende mudakererwa." "Ntukihebe Nyagasani azakomeza kukurinda."

### 6.6 Inziganyo (Conditional)
Buvuga ikintu cyashoboka haramutse habaye ikindi. Bushidikanya, buteganya, bukeka.
Irangwa n'uturemajambo **-a-**, ifite impindurantego **-a-ku-**.
- Examples:
  - "Twize twatsinda" → tu-a-ø-tsind-a
  - "Tutabonye uburyo ntitwakubaka" → nti-tu-a-ku-ubak-a
  - "Ugiye ukoresha ikoranabuhanga ryagufasha" → ri-a-ku-ø-fasha

## PART 7: IBIHE BY'INSHINGA (Verb Tenses)

### 7.1 Ibihe bikuru bitatu (Three main tenses)

#### Impitagihe (Past)
Two sub-types:
- **Impitakare** (recent past): ibikorwa bibaye mu kanya kashize/kare mu gitondo
  - Example: "Nakoze akazi mu gitondo."
- **Impitakera** (remote past): ibikorwa byabaye ejo/bihe byabanjirije ejo
  - Example: "Wagize amanota make usoza umwaka wa gatatu."

#### Indagihe (Present) — four sub-types:
1. **Indagihe y'aka kanya** (immediate present): happening right now
   - Example: "Umuganwa arasoma igitabo."
2. **Indagihe y'ubusanzwe** (habitual present): happens regularly
   - Example: "Giramahoro ahinga uburo n'imbogeri."
3. **Indagihe y'igikomezo** (progressive): action that started and continues
   - Example: "Igihe mwahereye muracyakora ubushakashatsi?"
4. **Indagihe mbarankuru** (narrative present): past events narrated as if present
   - Example: "Icyo gihe amapfa aratera Bakame irasonza."

#### Inzagihe (Future) — two sub-types:
- **Inzahato** (immediate future): will happen very soon (before end of day)
  - Example: "Nimugoroba turasura abasizwe iheruheru n'ibiza."
- **Inzakera** (remote future): will happen tomorrow or beyond
  - Example: "Mwige mushyizeho umwete muzatsinda neza ibizamini bya Leta."

## PART 8: NTERA (Adjective)

### 8.1 Inshoza (Definition)
Ntera ni ijambo rigaragira izina rigasobanura **imiterere, imimerere n'ingano** by'iryo zina. Ntera yegerana n'izina ifutura, cyangwa bigahuzwa n'inshinga **kuba, ni cyangwa si**.

### 8.2 Uturango (Characteristics)
- Ntera yisanisha n'izina biri kumwe igafata **indanganteko** yaryo ho indangasano
- Injyira mu nteko zose z'amazina
- Igaragaza indanganteko y'izina igaragiye iyo indanganteko yaryo itigaragaza

### 8.3 Intego (Structure)
**Formula: RS + C** (Indangasano + Igicumbi)

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| RS = Indangasano | Changes based on noun class of the noun it modifies; equals noun's indanganteko |
| C = Igicumbi | Does not change; carries the meaning |

**Ingero:**
- Umukinnyi **mushya** yatsinze ibitego byinshi → RS=mu, C=shya
- Umurima **mwiza** wera imyaka myinshi → RS=mu, C=iza (u→w/_J)
- Ubutunzi **bwiza** → bu-iza (u→w/_J)
- Intera **ndende** → n-re-n-re (r→d/n-)
- Imyaka **myinshi** → mi-inshi (i→y/_J)

### 8.4 Urutonde rw'ibicumbi bya ntera (Common adjective stems)

| Stem | Meaning | Note |
|------|---------|------|
| -nini | big | |
| -to | small | Reduplicates: gitoto, bifata RS ebyiri |
| -re | long | Reduplicates: kirekire |
| -gufi | short | Can add -ya: mugufiya |
| -ke | few/little | Can add -ya: makeya |
| -shya | new | Can reduplicate: mushyashya |
| -bi | bad | |
| -za | beautiful | |
| -eru | white/clean | |
| -ogo | deep/wide | |
| -nzima | heavy/difficult | |
| -rimbwa | beloved | |
| -niya | worthy of... | Many phonological variants |

**Special reduplication:**
- `-re` and `-to` reduplicate: `ki-re-ki-re` (kirekire), `ki-to-ki-to` (gitoto)
- `-gufi, -ke, -to` can add `-ya`: mugufiya, makeya
- `-shya, -to` can reduplicate: mushyashya, gitoto

### 8.5 Amategeko y'igenamajwi akoreshwa muri ntera
Same rules as for nouns: u→w/_J, i→y/_J, r→d/n-

## PART 9: IKINYAZINA (Pronouns)

### 9.1 Ikinyazina Nyereka (Demonstrative Pronouns)
Kerekana izina giherekeje cyangwa kikaryibutsa. Kigabanyijemo amacyiciro 6:
- **Icyiciro 1**: kintu kiri hafi cyane y'abavugana → igicumbi: **ø** (zero stem), ntego: D–Rkz–ø
- **Icyiciro 2**: kiri hafi y'uvuga → igicumbi: **-no**, ntego: Rkz–C
- **Icyiciro 3**: kiri hafi y'uwo babwira → igicumbi: **-o**, ntego: D–Rkz–C
- **Icyiciro 4**: kitaruye abavugana bombi → igicumbi: **-riya**, ntego: Rkz–C
- **Icyiciro 5**: kibukwa (previously known) → igicumbi: **-rya**, ntego: Rkz–C
- **Icyiciro 6**: baba barigeze kubona hamwe → igicumbi: **-âa**, ntego: Rkz–C

### 9.2 Ikinyazina Mbanziriza (Relative Pronoun)
Gihagararira izina ryagombaga kuba icyuzuzo k'inshinga. **Buri gihe kibanziriza inshinga**.
- Intego: D – Rkz – C (-ô)
- Mu mpine: D – Rkz – C
- Example: "Uwô nkunda ararwaye" (uwô = ikinyazina mbanziriza); cf. "Uwo mwana nkunda ararwaye" (uwo = nyereka)

### 9.3 Ikinyazina Nyamubaro (Numeral Pronoun)
Giherekeza izina kerekana **umubare** (1–7).
Ibicumbi: **-mwe, -biri, -tatu, -ne, -tanu, -tandatu, -rindwi**
Intego: Rkz + C

**Ikitonderwa:** Imibare irenze 7 ni amazina mu nteko 3 n'iya 6:
- Abantu umunani, ikenda (amazina; nt.3 ubumwe)

### 9.4 Ikinyazina Mpamagazi (Vocative Pronoun)
Ribanziriza izina, rihamagara umuntu/ikintu gihagarariwe n'iryo zina.
**Itegeko**: Izina ribanjirijwe n'ikinyazina mpamagazi **ritakaza indomo** iyo riyifite, kandi rikurikirwa n'ikinyazina ngenga.
- Example: "Mwa Banyarwanda mwe" (mwa=mpamagazi, mwe=ngenga)

### 9.5 Ikinyazina Ngenera (Associative/Possessive Pronoun)
Kugaragaza ubwigenge n'isano:
- Izina + ikinyazina ngenera + izina risobanura
- Ingero: umurima wacu, umwana wange, amafaranga yabo, ishati yawe

**Orthographic rule**: Ikinyazina ngenera gikurikiwe n'ikinyazina ngenga byandikwa mu ijambo rimwe.
- umwana **wange** → wa + nge = wange
- urimi **rwacu** → rwa + cu = rwacu

## PART 10: IMYANDIKIRE YEMEWE (Official Orthographic Rules)
Source: Amabwiriza ya Minisitiri no 001/2014

### 10.1 Amagambo Yandikwa AFATANYE (Written as one word)

**Ingingo ya 1:** Amazina y'inyunge (including proper names of inyunge type) written together.
- Umwihanduzacumu, Rugwizangoga, Umukangurambaga, Amayirabiri

**Ingingo ya 2:** Ibyungo "na" na "nka" + ibinyazina ngenga bivuga nyakuvuga na nyakubwirwa (ngenga ya 1 n'iya 2) → written as ONE word.
- nange, nawe, natwe, namwe; nkange, nkawe, nkatwe, nkamwe

**Ingingo ya 3:** Ikinyazina ngenera + ikinyazina ngenga → written as ONE word.
- wange, wawe, wacu, wanyu, wabo
- rwacu, rwawe, rwabo, rwanyu

**Ingingo ya 4:** Impakanyi "nta" + inshinga itondaguye → written TOGETHER.
- ntawurwaye, ntabatsinzwe, ntakibikora

**Ingingo ya 5:** Ibinyazina ngenga byo mu nteko ndangahantu "ho", "yo", "mo/mwo" + akajambo "ko" → TOGETHER with the verb they precede (UNLESS the verb is "ni" or "si").
- tumusangayo, tugenderako, yaguyemo, afataho
- BUT: Ni ho ngiye. / Si ko avuze. (separate when verb is ni/si)

**Ingingo ya 6:** Ijambo "ni" + inshinga y'integeko cyangwa inzagihe → written TOGETHER.
- nimukore, nimufate, nimugende

**Ingingo ya 7:** Amagambo yerekana ibihe → written as ONE word:
- nimunsi, nijoro (ninjoro), nimugoroba, ejobundi

**Ingingo ya 8:** "munsi" yerekana ahantu (under/below) → written as ONE word.
- "Imbeba yihishe munsi y'akabati."

**Ingingo ya 9:** Imigereka ndangahantu iremewe ku ndangahantu "i" → written together:
- imuhira, iheru, iburyo, ibumoso, ivure, ikambere, imbere, ibwami, inyuma
- Also: i + kinyazina ngenera "wa" + kinyazina ngenga → one word: iwacu, iwabo, iwawe

**Ingingo ya 10:** Amagambo afatiwe hamwe akarema inyumane y'umugereka, inyumane y'icyungo, inyumane y'irangamutima (from pronouns no longer recognized as separate) → written together.
- niko, nuko, kuri bwo... (frozen forms)

**Ingingo ya 11:** Amagambo ashingiye ku isubiramo → written together.
- babiribabiri, kacikaci, umwumwe

**Ingingo ya 12:** Ijambo "nyiri" in certain honorific names → together with following word.
- Nyiricyubahiro (BUT: "Nyiri ubwenge" is separate - still two words when not fossilized)

---

### 10.2 Amagambo Yandikwa ATANDUKANYE (Written as separate words)

**Ingingo ya 1:** Amagambo mfutuzi (modifiers) → separate from modified word.
- Inama Njyanama y'Akarere, Umuco nyarwanda, umutima muhanano

**Ingingo ya 2:** Amazina nteruro in proverbs/praise-names/ibivugo with more than 4 words → written separately, placed in quotation marks.
- "Inshyikanya ku mubiri ya Rugema ahica"
- "Saruhara rwa Nkomokomo umwami w'ibishwi n'ibisiga"

**Ingingo ya 3:** Ibyungo "na" na "nka" + ibinyazina ngenga bivuga nyakuvugwa (ngenga ya 3) → separate.
- na we, na ko, na bwo; nka bo, nka yo

**Ingingo ya 4:** Impakanyi "nta" + other word types (not conjugated verb) → separate.
- nta we, nta cyo, nta byo (but: ntawurwaye when with verb)

**Ingingo ya 5:** "ho", "yo", "mo/mwo", "ko" + "ni" or "si" → separate.
- Ni ho ngiye. Si ko avuze. Si mo yaguye.

**Ingingo ya 6:** Akajambo "ko" kunga inyangingo ebyiri → separate.
- Nizeye ko tuzatsinda. / Hari abivugiye ko ubwo bukwe...

**Ingingo ya 7:** "ngo" + "wa wundi", "bya bindi", "aho ngaho", "uwo nguwo" → two words.
- kugira ngo, wa wundi, bya bindi, aho ngaho, uwo nguwo

**Ingingo ya 8:** "ku" na "mu" → always separate from ikinyazina ngenera; also "ku wa" na "mu wa" before dates.
- ku bwabo, mu rugo; ku wa 12 Ugushyingo, ku wa Mbere

**Ingingo ya 9:** "(i)saa" + umubare → separate.
- saa tatu, i saa yine (not isaaytatu)

**Ingingo ya 10:** Indangahantu "i" + izina bwite ry'ahantu → separate.
- i Kirinda, i Muyunzwe, i Muhanga, i Kigali, i Rwanda, i Burayi

**Ingingo ya 11:** Inshinga mburabuzi "ari" in ngaragira (relative) constructions → separate from both surrounding words.
- umuntu uwo ari we wese / ibyo ari byo byose

**Ingingo ya 12:** Amagambo ahuje ishusho (demonstrative inyumane but still with referent) → separate.
- Uko arya ni ko akora. / Uku kwezi ni uko guhinga.

**Ingingo ya 13:** Amazina bwite y'ibihungu/uturere with indomo → indomo separate (lowercase unless sentence-initial).
- A Marangara, i Gisaka, U Bubirigi (proper place names)

**Ingingo ya 14:** Akabimbura "nyiri" (possessive, not frozen) + "ni" + "si" → separate from following noun.
- Nyiri ubwenge aruta nyiri uburyo. / Amasunzu si amasaka. / Umwana ni umutware.

## PART 11: IMYANDIKIRE Y'IBIHEKANE (Consonant Cluster Orthography)

Source: S5 SB, pp. 763–792

### 11.1 Complete list of Kinyarwanda consonant clusters (ibihekane):
```
Prenasalized:  mb, mf, mp, mv, nd, ng, nj, nk, ns, nsh, nshy, nt, nz
Labialized:    bw(bg), cw, dw, (fw), gw, hw, jw, kw, mw, nw, nyw, (pfw), (pw),
               rw, shw, shyw, sw, tsw, tw, (vw), zw
Palatalized:   by, cy, jy, my, nny, pfy, py, ry, sy, ty, (vy)
Mixed lab+pal: byw, myw, pfyw, (ryw), (vyw)
Prenasalized labialized: mbw, (mfw), mpw, (mvw), ndw, ngw, njw, nkw,
                          (nshw), (nshyw), (nsw), ntw, nzw
Prenasalized palatalized: mby, mpy, (mvy), ncy, ndy, njy, nsy, nty
Mixed all:     (mbyw), (mvyw), njyw
```
Note: Igihekane **bg** gikoreshwa gusa mu ijambo **Kabgayi**.

### 11.2 (n)kw, (n)gw, hw + inyajwi o cyangwa u → REPLACED

Rule: Ibihekane (n)kw, (n)gw, hw **before vowels o or u** are NOT written; replaced by (n)ko, (n)ku, (n)go, (n)gu, ho, hu.

```
Write:  kwi, ku, ko, kwa, kwe
        nkwi, nku, nko, nkwa, nkwe
        gwi, gu, go, gwa, gwe
        ngwi, ngu, ngo, ngwa, ngwe
        hwi, hu, ho, hwa, hwe
```

Examples: "Ngwije akunda koga" (not: Nkwije), "Ngoboka yahwituye Hondi"

### 11.3 (n)jy na (n)cy + inyajwi i cyangwa e → REPLACED

Rule: (n)jy and (n)cy only before a, o, u. Before i or e, write (n)gi, (n)ge, (n)ki, (n)ke.

```
Write:  ki, cyu, cyo, cya, ke
        nki, ncyu, ncyo, ncya, nke
        gi, jyu, jyo, jya, ge
        ngi, njyu, njyo, njya, nge
```

Examples: "Nkunda gusenga njya mu mugi" (not njya→njya is ok since njya = before a), "Iki kibo cyuzuye" (ki before vowel e→ke: "icyumba ke")

Note: Amazina bwite that were established before these rules keep their old spelling.
- Intara y'Amajyepfo, Umujyi wa Kigali, Akagari ka Cyimana (preserved)

### 11.4 ts, pf, c + inyamazuru → REPLACED

Rule: ts, pf, c preceded by inyamazuru (nasal) write as: ns, mf, nsh respectively.
- "Yohana yansikamiye" (not: yatsikamiye)
- Insina za kijyambere, Imfizi

## PART 12: IKATA N'ITAKARA RY'INYAJWI (Elision Rules)

### 12.1 Ikata ry'inyajwi (Vowel elision)

**Ibyungo "na" na "nka" + ibinyazina ngenera:** Final vowel of the conjunction is elided when followed by word beginning with consonant (or vowel in certain cases):
```
nk'umurwayi (nka + umurwayi → nk')
w'ibihumbi (wa + ibihumbi → w')
```

BUT: Final vowel is NOT elided when followed by a NUMBER (written in digits):
- "Umwaka w'ibihumbi bibiri na cumi n'ibiri" (na + 2012 → na 2012, no elision)
- "Umwaka wa 2017" (wa not elided before numeral)

### 12.2 Inyajwi zisoza amagambo ntizikatwa (Word-final vowels NOT elided):
- Regular word-final vowels are never elided
- Ingero: "Tuzatera imbuto imvura iguye." "Twubatse amashuri ateza imbere."

### 12.3 Inyajwi "i" isoza akabimbura nyiri, ni, si → NOT elided:
- nyiri, ni, si → their final -i is never cut
- "Nyiri ubwenge aruta nyiri uburyo." (nyiri stays intact)
- BUT: When nyiri is fossilized in a proper name → written together: Nyiricyubahiro

### 12.4 Inyajwi zisoza indangahantu "ku" na "mu" → NOT elided:
- ku, mu do not elide before other words
- BUT: inyajwi itangira amazina bwite / rusange akurikiye mu/ku DOES elide/drop:
  - "Basuye nyirasenge mu Mutara" (mu + Mutara: u of Mutara absorbed)
  - "Twanyuze ku Kagera" (ku + Kagera: K of Kagera takes the place)
  - Exception: Words starting with vowel i without class marker: ku + ishuri → ku ishuri (both words)

## PART 13: AMASAKU MBONEZANTERURO (Tonal Sandhi Rules)

Source: S3 SB pp. 354–375 and S5 SB pp. 560–596

### 13.1 Overview
Amasaku (tones) of words change when words are in a sentence, due to neighboring words.

Key tone types referenced:
- **Isaku nyejuru** (high tone) — marked ´ or â in notation
- **Isaku nyesi** (low tone) — unmarked or marked `
- **Isaku nyejuru nyesi** — rising tone ˆ or â notation

### 13.2 Rules for "na", "nka" and possessive pronouns (a-class)

1. When "na"/"nka"/possessive (igicumbi -a) + noun without indomo, but noun has high tone on 3rd syllable → noun takes **high tone on 1st syllable**
   - Kiizâ na Mûganwâ → Mûganwâ gets high on mu-

2. When "na"/"nka"/ngenera + noun without indomo, 2nd syllable has rising (nyejuru nyesi) tone → rising tone preserved, 1st syllable also gets high
   - Inzu ya Kâliîsa → Ka- gets high

3. When "na"/"nka"/ngenera + noun without indomo, 2nd syllable has high tone → tone shifts to 1st syllable
   - Mutôni na Gâsaro → high moves forward

4. When "na"/"nka"/ngenera (a-class) ELIDED (kaswe) + noun with indomo → indomo gets **high tone**
   - Umugorê n'ûmugabo → ûmugabo (umu- becomes high)
   - Abâana b'âbakoôbwa → â- prefix high

5. When above but noun has high/rising on 2nd syllable → indomo gets **rising tone (nyejuru nyesi)**
   - Afatwa nk'îintwâari → î- rising

6. When "na"/"nka"/ngenera (a-class) ELIDED + ikinyazina nyereka → nyereka takes **rising tone** on initial vowel

### 13.3 Additional tonal sandhi rules (S5)

- **Ikinyazina mbanziriza** always carries **high tone on its igicumbi**: Uwô mvuga, Ibyô akora
- **Indangahantu ho, yo, mo/mwo + inshinga** always carry **high tone**
- **Akajambo ko** (complementizer) always carries **high tone**
- **Inshinga mburabuzi "ni"/"si"** in a sentence always carry **high tone** (but loses it when sentence-initial)
  - Amasuunzu sî amasakâ. / Uwô nshâakâ nî uwo.
- **Indangahantu "i"** can also change adjacent tones
- **Ibyungo "no" na "nko" + ibinyazina ngenera (o-class)** always take **high tone**
  - Kuryâ nô kuryâama biraangana.

## PART 15: AMATIRANO — LOANWORD ADAPTATION

Source: S5 SB pp. 1335–1355

### 15.1 Amategeko ajyanye n'inshinga zatiwe
Borrowed verbs must follow Kinyarwanda verb structure:
- Must take indangashinga **ku-**
- Must take umusozo **-a**
- Examples:
  - kusoma (from Fr. "lire" → already native)
  - guterefona (téléphoner → ku-terefon-a)
  - kwiyaranja (s'arranger → ku-iyaranj-a)

### 15.2 Amategeko ajyanye n'izina zatiwe
Borrowed nouns take Kinyarwanda noun structure: D + RT + C (gafata inteko).

Most common pattern: **ø-ø-C** (no indomo, no indanganteko) → in nteko ya 9 (singular), nteko ya 10 (plural: za + C).

Can also take plural in nteko ya 6 (ama-):
- terefoni / za terefoni / amaterefoni
- tereviziyo / za tereviziyo / amatereviziyo
- radiyo / za radiyo / amaradiyo

If plural in nt.6 is used → gains indomo and indanganteko:
- ø-ø-terefoni (sg) → a-ma-terefoni (pl)

## PART 16: KEY PHONOLOGICAL RULES SUMMARY (Amategeko y'igenamajwi)

| Rule notation | Description | Example |
|---------------|-------------|---------|
| u→w/_J | u becomes w before another vowel (glide formation) | umwana = u-mu-ana |
| u→ø/_J | u drops before vowel in certain contexts | abana = a-ba-ana |
| i→y/_J | i becomes y before vowel | iyi=iyinshi → myinshi |
| i→e/_Co- | i becomes e after nasal+consonant | imbonekarimwe: i→e |

| n→m/_h; mh→mp | nasal+h → mp in writing | impuzamiryango |

## PART 17: IKIBONEZAMVUGO KURI NTERURO (Sentence-Level Grammar Notes)

### 17.1 Parts of sentence (imimaro y'interuro)
1. **Ruhamwa** (Subject): Ijambo rigaragaza ukora igikorwa/imimerere. Can be: izina, ikinyazina, imbundo.
2. **Inshinga** (Verb predicate): Ribumbatiye igikorwa/imimerere n'imico bya ruhamwa.
3. **Icyuzuzo** (Complement/Object): Amagambo aherekeza inshinga; types include:
   - Ruhamya (predicative complement)
   - Icyuzuzo mbonera (direct object)
   - Icyuzuzo nziguro (indirect/oblique)
   - Icyuzuzo k'impagike (pronominalized object, infixed in verb)

### 17.2 Ruhamwa mburebuzi
Ruhamwa mburebuzi igaragara igihe hakoreshejwe inshinga itagaragaza ijambo yisanisha na ryo. Ruhamwa igaragazwa n'indanganshinga. Ciyanecyane mu nshinga nkene zifite igicumbi -ri n'inshinga nshinga...

### 17.3 Ibisantera (Compounds/Idiomatic expressions)
Muri rusange ibisantera bigira uturemajambo dutandukanye bitewe n'aho bikomoka.
Example analysis:
- Ibihingwa ngengabukungu: n-geng-a-ø-bu-kung-u
- Ubutegetsi nyubahirizategeko: compound of several morphemes
